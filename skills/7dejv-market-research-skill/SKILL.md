# 7DEJV Market Research Skill

## Procedure
1. Define category, market, time window and allowed sources.
2. Gather comparable data for at least 10 candidates when possible.
3. Record source URLs, price, size, unit price, ratings, reviews, claims and visible sales signals.
4. Distinguish observed data from estimates.
5. Score demand, margin potential, differentiation, production difficulty and risk.
6. Return 3–5 candidates for deeper analysis.

## Output
Return structured candidates, evidence, assumptions, missing data, risks and statuses `TOP`, `OBSERVE`, `HOLD` or `REJECT`.

## Restrictions
Do not bypass access controls, fabricate sales counts, buy products or treat review volume as proven sales.
