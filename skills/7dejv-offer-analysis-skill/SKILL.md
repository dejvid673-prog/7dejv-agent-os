# 7DEJV Offer Analysis Skill

Compare 3–5 offers using their recorded sources.

## Steps
1. Compare function, price, unit price, dosage, packaging and reviews.
2. Separate verified facts from marketing claims.
3. Identify repeated customer problems and missing features.
4. Mark legal, safety and similarity risks.
5. Prepare a structured brief for the next stage.

## Output
Return a comparison matrix, findings, evidence, assumptions, missing data and risks.

## Limits
Do not reproduce competitor names, text, graphics or distinctive packaging. Do not treat sales copy as scientific evidence.
