# 7DEJV Dosage Test Planning Skill

## Steps
1. List the evidence required before testing.
2. Define control records, observation fields and review checkpoints.
3. Record environmental parameters and adverse-event fields.
4. Separate research notes, pilot review and approved customer instructions.
5. Route the plan to a qualified human reviewer.

## Output
Return documentation fields, review checkpoints, missing evidence, risks and status `READY FOR REVIEW`, `HOLD` or `BLOCKED`.

## Limits
Do not generate or approve customer-facing usage quantities. Do not replace laboratory, regulatory or safety review.
