# 7DEJV Product Release Audit Skill

## Gates
1. Evidence and claims.
2. Safety and required documentation.
3. Human-reviewed usage instructions.
4. Independent name, copy and visual identity.
5. Commercial and logistics readiness.
6. Production traceability and quality-control plan.

## Procedure
Check every gate, cite the supporting artifact and assign an owner to every unresolved action.

## Output
Return `PASS`, `HOLD` or `BLOCKED`, passed gates, failed gates, missing evidence, risks and conditions for another audit.

## Limits
Do not publish, order, manufacture or approve missing evidence. `PASS` requires explicit human approval.
