# 7DEJV Formulation Brief Skill

## Steps
1. Define the customer problem, target effect, product form and use conditions.
2. List required functions and candidate ingredient roles.
3. Prepare safe, economic and premium concept variants.
4. Record compatibility questions, regulatory risks and missing tests.

## Output
Return an R&D brief, concept variants, required evidence and status `LAB ONLY`, `HOLD` or `READY FOR LAB REVIEW`.

## Limits
Do not approve a production formula or claim laboratory validation.
