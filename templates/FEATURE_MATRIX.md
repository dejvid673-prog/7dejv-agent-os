## Rules

- Every required user-visible or functional outcome must have an acceptance criterion.
- `PASS` requires evidence.
- `PARTIAL`, `FAIL`, `TODO` and `UNVERIFIED` remain open work.
- A criterion may be removed only by an explicit scope decision recorded in project state/decisions.
- Previously accepted criteria affected by a later change must be regression-verified.
