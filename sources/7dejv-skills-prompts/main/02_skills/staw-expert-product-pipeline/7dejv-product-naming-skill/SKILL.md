# 7DEJV Product Naming Skill

## Steps
1. Extract approved function, audience and brand constraints.
2. Generate functional, premium and technical naming directions.
3. Check spelling, pronunciation, meaning and similarity to known competitor names supplied in the record.
4. Reject confusing or overly similar options.
5. Return a short rationale for each candidate.

## Output
Return proposed names with `LOW RISK`, `REVIEW` or `REJECT` and a recommended shortlist.

## Limits
Do not claim trademark clearance. Do not create names by making minor edits to a competitor name.
