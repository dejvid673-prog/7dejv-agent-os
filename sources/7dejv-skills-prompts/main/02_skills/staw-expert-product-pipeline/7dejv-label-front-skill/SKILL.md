# 7DEJV Label Front Skill

## Steps
1. Select approved brand, product name, function, yield and pack-size fields.
2. Define information hierarchy for shelf and marketplace thumbnail readability.
3. Produce an original layout brief aligned with STAW EXPERT identity.
4. Check claim consistency and similarity risk.
5. Route the brief to the graphic builder and auditor.

## Output
Return label copy, layout zones, format assumptions, required assets, risks and status `DRAFT` or `READY FOR GRAPHIC AUDIT`.

## Limits
Do not imitate a competitor's distinctive layout, icons, colors or packaging. Do not treat a concept as print-ready artwork.
